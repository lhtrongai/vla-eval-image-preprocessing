\begin{abstract}
Vision-language-action (VLA) policies are compared by their success rates on simulated benchmarks such as LIBERO, often by margins of a few points. We ask how stable the outcome of a single episode is when the evaluation changes slightly. Before a policy sees a camera frame, the evaluator processes it with image steps copied from the training data pipeline, and a re-implementation can change any of them without raising an error. On all \NPairs{} initial states of LIBERO-Spatial we evaluate two released OpenVLA-family checkpoints, OpenVLA-7B and OpenVLA-OFT, change one of four image steps at a time, and pair every episode with the reference episode that starts from the same state (\MainEpisodes{} episodes in \NCells{} cells). No net change is resolved: turning off the JPEG round trip changes OpenVLA-7B by \SevJpegDiff{} points (\AlphaPct\% interval [\SevJpegLo, \SevJpegHi]; the one-sided bound on the loss, \SevJpegUB{} points, does not rule out the \ThresholdPts{} points fixed in advance), and no secondary comparison is significant after Holm correction. Individual outcomes move a lot: every step flips \SevFlipsMin{} to \SevFlipsMax{} of the \NPairs{} OpenVLA-7B episodes in both directions, while an identical rerun flips \SevRerunFlips. As controls fixed in a public plan before they ran, we applied meaningless changes as large, in intensity levels, as the two mildest steps: \ShamWhat. They flip \ShamSevRange{} OpenVLA-7B episodes, \ShamSevRatioPct\% of the flips of those two steps above the rerun floor. For OpenVLA-7B, most flips of the mild steps therefore reflect sensitivity to any change of about one intensity level, not an effect of the particular step, and a comparison between two evaluations needs a floor measured with such a change, not only with an identical rerun. OpenVLA-OFT flips far less (\ShamOftRange{} under the controls, \OftFlipsMin{} to \OftFlipsMax{} under the steps), and its steps, the crop above all, lose episodes that the controls do not. The primary comparison was written down before the full variant cells but after a pilot on \PilotPairs{} of the same states, without a public timestamp (\OutDiff{} points on the \OutPairs{} states outside the pilot). We release the per-episode logs, the evaluation code and the analysis scripts, which regenerate every number in the paper.
\end{abstract}
